The Ant and the Jar

A two-minute animated short.

A little boy ant lives with his parents in a nest inside King Solomon's castle. His father keeps telling him to follow good people. His mother tells him to be careful. The boy is tired of being corrected, decides he is right, and sets out to tell Solomon himself.

It is a story about a child who cannot yet tell good from bad, and a warning he did not listen to. I will not say how it ends.

The look is 2D, drawn characters with depth, in the manner of old pen-and-ink Bible engravings. I use AI tools to make the stills, then move the camera and cut the film in an editor. I would rather say that plainly than hide it.

Making of

Dated notes, including what is not working yet.

6 Oct 2026

Ending decided. I wanted it open for a while, so the audience could finish it themselves. Then I chose the harder one, because the story only works if the father was right. Wrote the script as ten shots with timing, camera and sound for each. Generated the first ten keyframes overnight.

6 Oct 2026

Known problems with the first keyframes: the ants drift slightly from frame to frame, one frame lost the father shielding the boy, one boy is smiling when he should not be, and one head outline is fuzzy. These are the ones to redo.

5 Oct 2026

Started. Two minutes, one story, one idea: the praise of ants in Proverbs turned into a boy's grievance. Reference I keep returning to: Chris Marker's La Jetee, a film made almost entirely of stills.

Next

Review the keyframes, redo the weak ones, then assemble the film in an editor. Nothing else is announced. More films and notes will be added here as they exist.
